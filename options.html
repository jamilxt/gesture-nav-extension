Two-Finger Gesture Navigation

Navigate back and forward with two-finger swipes

How it works: Swipe right with two fingers to go back, swipe left to go forward. Just like on macOS!

⚠️ Known Limitations

  • Chrome Internal Pages: This extension does not work on chrome:// URLs (New Tab, Settings, Extensions, etc.) due to Chrome security restrictions.
  • Navigation History: Gestures only work when there is browser history to navigate. If you're on the first page of a session, "back" won't do anything.
